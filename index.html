What this version tests
  • Uses past-frame, translation-compensated temporal motion to propose multiple candidate regions.
  • Runs Pose Landmarker on motion candidates, tracking-guided regions, and large plus smaller fallback tiles.
  • Chooses the best candidate by pose structure, visibility, torso reliability, center bias, and past-only tracking consistency—not by motion alone or first detection.
  • Tracking predicts from recent past positions, expands when confidence falls, and can broadly reacquire.
  • Produces a local, downloadable JSON diagnostic report. Video pixels and video files are never uploaded or retained.
Everything runs locally in Safari. There is no video-upload code. V7 is a diagnostic experiment, not the final chest-line timing algorithm.
